# 🌍 Chapter 8: MARPOL Annex VI

---

## 8.1 Introduction

MARPOL Annex VI is an international regulation that controls **air pollution from ships**.

It is part of the MARPOL Convention adopted by the IMO.

---

## 8.2 Objectives

* Reduce air pollution from ships
* Control greenhouse gas emissions
* Limit harmful exhaust gases
* Promote cleaner fuels

---

## 8.3 Controlled Emissions

### 🧪 Nitrogen Oxides (NOx)

Produced by marine diesel engines.

### 🧪 Sulphur Oxides (SOx)

Related to sulphur content in fuel oil.

### 🧪 Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Main greenhouse gas from combustion.

---

## 8.4 Fuel Oil Regulations

* Sulphur limit: 0.50% global cap
* 0.10% in Emission Control Areas (ECA)
* Use of low sulphur fuel or scrubbers

---

## 8.5 Energy Efficiency Measures

Annex VI includes:

* EEDI (design phase efficiency)
* EEXI (existing ships)
* CII (operational rating system)
* SEEMP (energy management plan)

---

## 8.6 Carbon Intensity Indicator (CII)

Ships are rated:

* A: Excellent performance
* B: Good performance
* C: Moderate
* D: Poor
* E: Very poor (corrective actions required)

---

## 8.7 Data Collection Systems

* MRV (EU system)
* DCS (IMO system)

Used to monitor fuel consumption and emissions.

---

## 8.8 Ship Energy Efficiency Management Plan (SEEMP)

Mandatory plan including:

* Operational efficiency measures
* Monitoring procedures
* Improvement strategies

---

## 8.9 Inspections and Compliance

MARPOL Annex VI is verified by:

* Flag State
* Recognized Organizations (RO)
* Port State Control (PSC)

---

## 8.10 Key Concept

> MARPOL Annex VI is the backbone of modern maritime decarbonization policy.
