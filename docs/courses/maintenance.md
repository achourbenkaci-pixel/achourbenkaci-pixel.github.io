# 🔧 Chapter 6: Ship Maintenance

---

## 6.1 Definition

Ship maintenance refers to all technical activities required to keep a vessel in **safe, reliable, and efficient operating condition**.

---

## 6.2 Objectives of Maintenance

* Ensure safety of navigation
* Prevent machinery failures
* Reduce downtime
* Optimize operational cost
* Comply with classification and flag requirements

---

## 6.3 Types of Maintenance

### 🔴 Corrective Maintenance

Performed after a failure occurs.

### 🟡 Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance based on time or running hours.

### 🟢 Predictive Maintenance

Based on condition monitoring and diagnostics.

---

## 6.4 Planned Maintenance System (PMS)

The PMS is a computerized system used onboard to:

* Schedule maintenance tasks
* Track equipment history
* Generate reports
* Ensure compliance with class requirements

---

## 6.5 Dry Docking

Dry docking is a mandatory periodic inspection where the ship is taken out of water for:

* Hull inspection
* Propeller and rudder checks
* Thickness measurement
* Major repairs

---

## 6.6 Main Machinery Maintenance

Includes:

* Main engine overhaul
* Auxiliary engines
* Pumps and compressors
* Boilers and heat exchangers

---

## 6.7 Failure Analysis

Common methods:

* Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
* Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)
* Condition monitoring

---

## 6.8 Spare Parts Management

Good maintenance requires:

* Proper inventory control
* Critical spare identification
* Timely procurement

---

## 6.9 Key Concept

> Good maintenance ensures safety, reliability, and economic operation of the vessel.
