# 🚢 Chapter 1: Introduction to Shipping

---

## 1.1 What is Shipping?

Shipping refers to the **transport of goods and passengers by sea** using commercial vessels.

It is the backbone of international trade, as more than **80% of global trade** is carried by maritime transport.

---

## 1.2 Importance of Shipping

Shipping is essential because:

* 🌍 It connects global markets
* 📦 It transports bulk cargo at low cost
* ⚙️ It supports industrial supply chains
* 🛢 It carries energy resources (oil, gas)
* 🚢 It enables international passenger transport

---

## 1.3 Main Actors in Shipping

### ⚓ Shipowner

The person or company that owns the vessel.

### 📊 Ship Manager (Technical Manager)

Responsible for:

* Maintenance
* Crew management
* Technical operation
* Safety compliance

### 📦 Charterer

The party that hires the ship to transport cargo.

### 🧭 Ship Agent

Represents the ship in port operations.

### 🏗 Cargo Owner

The entity that owns the goods being transported.

---

## 1.4 Types of Ships

### 🚢 Bulk Carriers

Transport unpackaged bulk cargo (grain, coal, ore).

### 🛢 Tankers

Transport liquids (oil, chemicals, LNG).

### 📦 Container Ships

Transport standardized containers.

### 🚗 Ro-Ro Ships

Transport vehicles (cars, trucks).

### ❄ LNG Carriers

Transport liquefied natural gas at low temperature.

---

## 1.5 Global Maritime Transport System

The shipping industry is structured around:

* International Maritime Organization (IMO)
* Flag States
* Port States
* Classification Societies (IACS members)

---

## 1.6 Major Maritime Routes

* Europe ↔ Asia (via Suez Canal)
* Asia ↔ America (Pacific routes)
* Europe ↔ America (Atlantic routes)
* Middle East ↔ Global oil routes

---

## 1.7 Key Concept: Economy of Scale

Large ships reduce transport cost per unit of cargo:

> The bigger the ship, the lower the cost per ton transported.

---

## 1.8 Summary

Shipping is a **global, complex, and regulated system** involving multiple actors and international conventions.

It is the foundation of maritime engineering and ship management studies.
