# 🧪 Chapter 9: Exercises & Case Studies

---

## 9.1 Objective

This section is designed to help students apply theoretical knowledge to **real maritime situations**.

---

## 9.2 Case Study 1 — PSC Detention

A bulk carrier is detained in port due to:

* Fire door not closing properly
* Expired liferaft certificates
* Oil leakage in engine room

### Questions:

* What are the PSC actions?
* Which conventions are violated?
* What corrective actions are required?

---

## 9.3 Case Study 2 — Engine Failure

A main engine experiences sudden shutdown during navigation.

### Possible causes:

* Fuel contamination
* Lubrication failure
* Turbocharger malfunction

### Questions:

* What is your troubleshooting procedure?
* What is the role of PMS in preventing this failure?

---

## 9.4 Case Study 3 — Energy Efficiency

A container ship has a CII rating of **D** for two consecutive years.

### Questions:

* What corrective actions should the company take?
* How can SEEMP be improved?
* What operational measures can reduce emissions?

---

## 9.5 Case Study 4 — MARPOL Annex VI

A vessel is found using fuel with 1.2% sulphur in a 0.5% regulated zone.

### Questions:

* What violation occurred?
* What penalties can apply?
* What systems should have prevented this?

---

## 9.6 Exercise — Classification Issue

During survey, cracks are found in hull structure.

### Questions:

* What is the role of the Classification Society?
* Can the ship lose class?
* What is the link with Flag State?
