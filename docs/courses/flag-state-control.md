# 🏴 Chapter 2: Flag State Control

---

## 2.1 Definition

Flag State Control refers to the **authority and responsibility of a State over ships registered under its flag**.

Every ship must sail under the flag of a country and is subject to its laws and regulations.

---

## 2.2 Responsibilities of the Flag State

The Flag State is responsible for ensuring that ships comply with:

* International conventions (IMO rules)
* Safety standards (SOLAS)
* Environmental regulations (MARPOL)
* Crew training standards (STCW)

---

## 2.3 Certification of Ships

Flag States ensure that vessels carry valid certificates such as:

* Safety Construction Certificate
* Safety Equipment Certificate
* Safety Radio Certificate
* International Load Line Certificate
* International Oil Pollution Prevention (IOPP)

---

## 2.4 Delegation to Recognized Organizations (RO)

Flag States often delegate inspections and certification to:

* Classification Societies
* Recognized Organizations (RO)

However, **the Flag State remains legally responsible**.

---

## 2.5 Flag State Inspection (FSI)

Flag State may conduct inspections to verify:

* Ship condition
* Crew compliance
* Certificate validity
* Safety management system

---

## 2.6 Relationship with Port State Control (PSC)

* Flag State ensures compliance BEFORE operation
* PSC inspects ships IN foreign ports
* PSC can detain ships if serious deficiencies are found

---

## 2.7 Key Concept

> The Flag State is the “legal parent” of the ship.

It is responsible for everything happening onboard from a regulatory perspective.
