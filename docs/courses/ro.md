# 🧾 Chapter 4: Recognized Organizations (RO)

---

## 4.1 Definition

Recognized Organizations (RO) are organizations authorized by a **Flag State** to perform statutory surveys and certification on its behalf.

In most cases, these organizations are Classification Societies.

---

## 4.2 Why RO exist

Flag States do not always have enough technical resources. Therefore, they delegate:

* Surveys
* Inspections
* Certification

to Recognized Organizations.

---

## 4.3 Main responsibilities of RO

RO are responsible for:

* Statutory surveys
* Issuing certificates on behalf of Flag State
* Verifying compliance with IMO conventions
* Reporting deficiencies to Flag State

---

## 4.4 Limitations of RO

Even if tasks are delegated:

* ⚠ The Flag State remains legally responsible
* RO cannot change international law
* RO must follow Flag State instructions

---

## 4.5 Relationship with Classification Societies

Many RO are also Classification Societies such as:

* DNV
* Bureau Veritas
* Lloyd's Register
* American Bureau of Shipping

---

## 4.6 RO vs Flag State vs PSC

| Entity     | Role                               |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Flag State | Legal authority of the ship        |
| RO         | Technical execution of inspections |
| PSC        | External inspection in port        |

---

## 4.7 Key Concept

> RO act as technical representatives of the Flag State but do not replace it.
