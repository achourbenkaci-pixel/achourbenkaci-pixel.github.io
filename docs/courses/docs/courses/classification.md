# 🏢 Chapter 3: Classification Societies

---

## 3.1 Definition

Classification Societies are organizations that establish and apply **technical rules for the design, construction, and maintenance of ships**.

Their main goal is to ensure the **structural safety and reliability** of vessels.

---

## 3.2 Role of Classification Societies

They are responsible for:

* Setting technical rules (Rules for Ships)
* Conducting surveys and inspections
* Issuing class certificates
* Monitoring ship condition throughout its life

---

## 3.3 Main Classification Societies (IACS members)

* DNV
* Bureau Veritas
* Lloyd's Register
* American Bureau of Shipping
* ClassNK

These societies are members of the **International Association of Classification Societies (IACS)**.

---

## 3.4 Classification Notation

A ship receives a **class notation** such as:

* ✠ Hull
* ✠ Machinery
* Oil Tanker
* Container Ship
* Ice Class
* AUT-UMS (Unattended Machinery Space)

---

## 3.5 Surveys and Inspections

Classification surveys include:

* ⚓ Annual Survey
* ⚓ Intermediate Survey
* ⚓ Special Survey (every 5 years)
* ⚓ Docking Survey

---

## 3.6 Class Maintenance Conditions

To maintain class, the ship must:

* Remain seaworthy
* Pass periodic inspections
* Repair deficiencies within deadlines

---

## 3.7 Relationship with Flag State and PSC

* Classification = Technical safety
* Flag State = Legal compliance
* PSC = External control in port

They are interconnected but independent systems.

---

## 3.8 Key Concept

> Classification ensures the ship is **technically safe to operate at sea**.
